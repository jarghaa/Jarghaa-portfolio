The opportunity

A growing segment of buyers in Nepal and across South Asia wants bespoke, durable, locally made pieces over imported flat-pack — for homes, hotels and offices.

  • Demand for craft is rising while supply of genuine handmade work stays thin.
  • The brand and the quality standard are already established.
  • Capacity, showroom and distribution can scale with the right partner.

Why this founder

Arpan Jargha Magar has spent fifteen years running operations and teams — keeping corporate networks alive at Websurfer Fiber Internet, growing a young estate agency at UK Sansar, and managing property at Dokohomes — before founding Jarghaa Furniture in 2025. He runs the workshop himself.
