From network cables to fine furniture

I have spent my career learning how things are built — networks, property portfolios, and now, objects made to outlast the people who buy them.

I started in information technology, keeping corporate networks alive at Websurfer Fiber Internet and selling software services at Proshore. That taught me systems thinking: how small parts fail, and how to keep them running.

Then came property — first as administrator and general manager at Dokohomes, then as admin manager at UK Sansar Estate Agency, where I helped a young agency grow into residential construction, deal sourcing, HMO management, serviced accommodation, and development.

In June 2025 I founded Jarghaa Furniture in Lalitpur. It brings everything together: reading what customers need, negotiating, running teams, and holding a standard of quality you can only reach by hand.

Off the pitch

Football has taught me as much about leading people as any role I have held.

I have supported Liverpool since the Brendan Rodgers years — a period that included a genuine title challenge and more than its share of disappointment. Jürgen Klopp's arrival changed the club's direction. He set out to turn doubters into believers, and he did it by rebuilding the squad, the culture, and the standards expected of everyone at the club.

Arne Slot then delivered the club's 20th league title in 2024-25, sealed with a 5-1 win over Tottenham at Anfield — the first won in front of supporters there since 1990. The following season proved harder, and the club changed direction again, appointing Andoni Iraola in June 2026.

Build, peak, decline, rebuild. Anyone who has run a company will recognise the pattern. It is the same discipline in a different arena.

The players and managers I have admired most:

  • Steven Gerrard — captain, and the standard I measure leaders against
  • Philippe Coutinho — creativity in the tightest spaces
  • Jürgen Klopp — the manager who rebuilt the club
  • Trent Alexander-Arnold — homegrown, and a decade of service
  • Mohamed Salah — consistent in the decisive moments
  • Roberto Firmino — the link that held the attack together

Following a club for this long is a lesson in patience: the rebuilding matters as much as the winning.
